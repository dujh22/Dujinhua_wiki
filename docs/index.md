## Research Interests

- **Meta-Learning and Transfer Learning for Large Models**: Investigating methods for transferring and adapting learning strategies across different tasks and domains
- **Adaptive and Interpretable AI Systems**: Exploring how to build intelligent systems that flexibly adapt to environmental changes and are more transparent and interpretable to human users

## Honors & Life
